  • 1Connection card shows the system, space/project, and a live status pill — trust signal before anything is fetched.
  • 2Checkable results for Jira issues and Confluence pages, pre-selected by relevance so most users just confirm and go.
  • 3Fetch console. The one deliberate risk in this flow — turns an invisible MCP call into a visible, timestamped log. Builds confidence in what's being pulled.
